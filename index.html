FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About ZenSpend

Have questions about how ZenSpend protects your privacy and simplifies your daily budget? Find your answers below.

Does ZenSpend work without an internet connection?
Yes, 100%! ZenSpend is engineered from the ground up as a native offline app. Every single feature—including logging transactions, tracking daily budget limits, viewing charts, and exporting backups—works completely without an internet connection, mobile data, or Wi-Fi.
Where is my financial data stored?
Your financial data is stored exclusively on your phone's internal device storage. It is never transmitted to any cloud server, third-party analytics provider, or remote database. You have 100% data sovereignty.
How does the 1-Tap Quick Tab Logger work?
Quick Tabs allow you to save your frequent daily purchases (like ₹30 Tea, ₹120 Lunch, or ₹40 Commute). Instead of opening a form and typing the details each time, you simply tap the chip on your home screen to log the expense instantly in under 1 second.
How does the Daily Spending Limit work?
You can set your desired daily spending allowance in Settings (e.g. ₹500/day or $20/day). The Today Spotlight bar on your home screen visualizes your daily spending in real-time with color-coded bars: Green when you are safely on track, Amber when approaching your limit, and Red if you exceed your daily allowance.
Can I transfer my data to a new phone?
Yes! ZenSpend includes a complete Data Backup & Restore manager. You can export a clean JSON or CSV file to your phone's Downloads folder, Google Drive, or email, and import it into your new phone in seconds with smart duplicate detection.
What currencies does ZenSpend support?
ZenSpend supports all major global currencies including INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), CAD (CA$), AUD (AU$), and more. You can change your preferred currency anytime in Settings with automatic symbol formatting across the entire app.
Is ZenSpend free to use?
Yes, ZenSpend is free to download and use on Android. All core tracking, daily limits, quick tabs, and backup features are fully accessible without subscription paywalls.
